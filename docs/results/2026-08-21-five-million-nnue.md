# Five-Million-Position NNUE Baseline — 2026-08-21

## Decision

Keep classical evaluation as the default. The five-million-position experiment
completed end to end without a data, export, C++ agreement, crash, or illegal
move failure, but no tested network established a playing-strength gain over
the classical evaluator.

This is a successful production-pipeline milestone and a rejected strength
candidate. Network files and match artifacts remain outside Git because they
are generated evidence, not source code.

## Data and provenance

Sixteen Stockfish 18 shards sampled the July 2026 Lichess standard-rated CC0
archive. Six concurrent teachers used two threads, 256 MiB hash, and 20,000
nodes per sampled position. Positions came from plies 8–180 with whole-game
validation assignment and global position deduplication.

| Split | Retained positions | Size | SHA-256 |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Training | 5,000,000 | 210,000,024 bytes | `560a7dbc0b3e4a75ea3dbc1fa707a70485f3691fbbae3acbf224e4581d16c208` |
| Validation | 616,632 | 25,898,568 bytes | `6ff96ade7525f345fbea59255edd301d01a348495603e9ff8e001c2acb294db7` |

The merge read 5,131,236 training candidates and skipped 131,236 duplicates.
It read 641,380 validation candidates and skipped 24,748 duplicates. The source
archive was 29,051,046,504 bytes with SHA-256
`68738b1c448f051dc8d42db645d5b01749988a3bc1c24981adfe44ea92060dc7`.
Labelling ran from 2026-08-20 20:31 to 2026-08-21 10:58 local time. The final
resume/merge pass was generated by clean commit `aeb465b`.

The training audit saw 37,890/40,960 HalfKP inputs (92.50%), up from 71.83% in
the earlier 158,699-position corpus. Coverage is still highly uneven: 3,070
inputs were unseen, 21,184 occurred at most 100 times, and the median frequency
among seen features was 114. This provides a concrete target for future
sampling rather than assuming that raw position count alone is sufficient.

## Original centipawn baseline

The fixed 256-wide HalfKP-v1 model trained on an RTX 3070, early-stopped, was
quantized, and passed exact Python/C++ verification. Its subsequent 400-game
paired match against classical used the same executable on both sides, UHO
openings with reversed colours, one thread, 256 MiB hash, and `10+0.1`.

| Candidate wins | Classical wins | Draws | Score | Relative Elo |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 102 | 242 | 56 | 32.5% | -127.0 +/- 33.4 |

There were no time forfeits, crashes, illegal moves, or disconnects. The
network was decisively rejected.

## WDL objective and result blending

Commit `d9eb700` added an opt-in probability-space WDL objective. Stockfish
centipawns are mapped through a calibrated logistic function; optional game
results are blended in probability space rather than pretending that a result
is a made-up centipawn value. Existing centipawn-Huber training remains the
default for compatibility.

Three otherwise controlled candidates tested result weights 0.00, 0.15, and
0.30. All used a 400 cp WDL scale, 256 hidden units, seed 1, batch size 2,048,
cosine scheduling, and early stopping. The result-free candidate was best in
the playing screens. It selected epoch 3 with validation Brier/loss 0.010603,
200.78 cp MAE, 433.21 cp RMSE, and 84.12% sign accuracy across all 616,632
validation positions.

The selected network's quantized export measured 1.060 cp RMSE and 3.668 cp
maximum error over 1,024 sampled positions, then exactly matched C++ on the
four verification positions. Network SHA-256:
`c22959977925e9585c90a886cec58ee649fcc6f1c21d23df1dd2c6b26e8f4126`.

Pure-NNUE 40-game screens:

| Candidate | Wins | Losses | Draws | Score | Relative Elo |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Result weight 0.00 | 15 | 20 | 5 | 43.8% | -43.7 +/- 104.4 |
| Result weight 0.15 | 12 | 20 | 8 | 40.0% | -70.4 +/- 100.7 |
| Result weight 0.30 | 10 | 24 | 6 | 32.5% | -127.0 +/- 109.3 |

These samples only selected the least-poor candidate; none is large enough to
support an Elo claim.

## Classical/NNUE blending

Commit `1b1c412` added a tested `NNUE Weight` UCI option (`0..100`). It performs
a signed linear blend of classical and NNUE centipawn scores, with pure NNUE at
100. Classical evaluation remains the global default, and the original pure
NNUE behaviour is unchanged when the option is not adjusted.

Forty-game screens of the selected WDL candidate produced:

| NNUE weight | Wins | Losses | Draws | Score | Relative Elo |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 25 | 14 | 14 | 12 | 50.0% | 0.0 +/- 92.0 |
| 50 | 16 | 15 | 9 | 51.2% | +8.7 +/- 97.1 |

The apparent lead at weight 50 has much greater uncertainty than its effect
size, so it triggered a fresh-seed 200-game confirmation rather than promotion.

The confirmation used seed 47, the same frozen `1b1c412` executable on both
sides, `10+0.1`, UHO paired openings, one thread, 256 MiB hash, and concurrency
four. It completed all 200 games:

| Hybrid wins | Classical wins | Draws | Score | Relative Elo | LOS |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 76 | 82 | 42 | 48.5% | -10.4 +/- 43.0 | 31.7% |

The hybrid scored 52.0% as White and 45.0% as Black. There were no time
forfeits, crashes, illegal moves, or disconnects. This larger fresh-seed sample
did not reproduce the small screen's apparent lead and gives no basis for
promotion.

## Throughput

Fixed-depth-8 measurements used the frozen `1b1c412` Windows Release binary:

| Evaluator | NPS |
|---|---:|
| Classical | 62,682 |
| 25% NNUE blend | 31,311 |
| 50% NNUE blend | 33,728 |
| Pure selected NNUE | 48,020 |

Blending evaluates both backends and roughly halves throughput. A blend must
therefore provide a substantial evaluation-quality gain to recover its search
cost; a statistically neutral result would still be unsuitable as a default.

## Next technical direction

Do not spend more compute merely repeating the same architecture and targets.
The next controlled NNUE iteration should address both long-tail coverage and
model capacity or representation:

1. deliberately oversample the 3,070 unseen and 21,184 low-frequency inputs;
2. prototype a stronger two-perspective hidden stack or HalfKA-style inputs;
3. retain the WDL loss as an experimental option and retest target calibration;
4. benchmark inference before any long match; and
5. require a paired gain over classical before promotion.

The classical engine is still the release candidate. NNUE remains an optional,
fully verified research backend.
